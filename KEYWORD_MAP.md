## 3. SERP Content Mapping Rules

1. **Title Length:** Maximum 60 characters to prevent truncation on mobile screens (viewport 360px–412px).
2. **Meta Description:** Maximum 155 characters with a compelling Call-To-Action ("Visit us", "Call now", "Explore menu").
3. **Headings:**
   - Single canonical `<h1>` per page.
   - `<h2>` sections clearly demarcating Cuisines, About, Customer Reviews, Amenities, and FAQs.
   - `<h3>` items for specific dishes and features.
4. **Internal Linking Anchor Text:**
   - Replace generic "Click here" or "View" with keyword-descriptive anchors: `"Explore Full Pure Veg Menu"`, `"Read Our Solapur Culinary Story"`, `"Get Directions to Sakhar Peth Restaurant"`.
