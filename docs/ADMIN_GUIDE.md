# Amol Veg Resto — Restaurant Owner's Guide

Welcome to your restaurant management dashboard! This guide is written specifically for you and your staff. It explains how to easily manage your food menus, customer QR codes, website photos, and reviews without needing any technical knowledge.

---

## 1. How to Log In to Your Dashboard

You can access your admin dashboard from any device — a laptop, desktop, iPad, or your mobile phone.

1. Open your browser and go to your website address followed by `/admin` (for example: `https://your-restaurant-website.com/admin`).
2. Enter your registered email address and password.
3. Tap **Sign In**.
4. You will immediately arrive at your restaurant dashboard.

> 💡 **Tip for Phones**: You can add this page to your phone's home screen ("Add to Home Screen" in Chrome or Safari) so it opens like an app with one tap!

---

## 2. Understanding Your Dashboard & QR Numbers

When you log in, the dashboard gives you a quick snapshot of how your restaurant is performing:

- **Total Scans Today & This Week**: Shows how many customers opened your digital menu by scanning the table QR code.
- **Busiest Hours of the Day**: A chart showing what time of day most customers are viewing your menu (e.g. Lunch 12 PM–3 PM or Dinner 7 PM–10 PM). This helps you plan kitchen staffing and prep.
- **Customer Devices**: Shows whether your guests are using Android or iPhone to read the menu.
- **Review Conversion**: Shows how many guests tapped the "Review Us" button and posted a Google review.

## 3. Managing Your Food Menus

Your dashboard lets you control every item on your menu in real time. Any change you make appears on your customers' screens within seconds.

### Adding a New Dish
1. In the navigation, tap **Menus** and make sure the **Dishes** tab is selected.
2. Tap the orange **+ Add New Dish** button.
3. Fill in the dish details:
   - **Dish Name**: e.g., *Special Solapuri Kaju Curry*.
   - **Category**: Select where it belongs (Starters, Main Course, Breads, Beverages, or Desserts).
   - **Description**: A short, appetizing description.
   - **Pricing Type**: Choose **Single Price** (e.g. ₹ 220) or **Variants** (e.g. Half: ₹ 130, Full: ₹ 220).
   - **Dietary & Spice Level**: Choose Veg or Jain Available, and select the spice level (Mild, Medium, or Spicy).
   - **Special Badge**: Add a highlight badge like *Chef's Special* or *Must Try*.
   - **Dish Photo**: Tap **Upload Photo** to pick a photo from your phone or computer.
4. Tap **Save Dish**. It is now live on your digital menu!

### Marking a Dish as "Sold Out"
During a busy shift when an item runs out of stock:
1. Go to **Menus** > **Dishes**.
2. Find the dish in the list.
3. Toggle the switch to **Sold Out**.
4. On your digital menu, the dish card will immediately show a gentle "Sold Out" tag and disable ordering so customers are not disappointed. When fresh ingredients arrive, simply toggle it back on!

### Editing or Deleting a Dish
- Tap the **Edit (Pencil)** icon next to any dish to change its price, description, or photo.
- Tap the **Trash** icon to remove a dish.

## 4. How the Two Menus Work (Main Menu vs Special Dining Menu)

Your restaurant has two separate menus:
1. **Main Menu**: The regular daily menu customers see when scanning the standard QR code.
2. **Special Dining Menu**: An exclusive menu with slightly elevated pricing, ideal for special dining sections, weekends, or private events.

### How Dynamic Pricing Works:
- You don't have to manually calculate prices for the Special Menu!
- By default, the system automatically adds a small percentage (e.g. 10%) to your regular prices and rounds them cleanly to the nearest whole rupee.
- **Manual Overrides**: If you want a specific dish to have a custom special price (for example, keeping your signature dish at a fixed price), you can enter a "Special Price Override" directly on that dish.

## 5. Updating the Looping Menu Video

Your digital menu features an appetizing looping video at the top:
1. Go to **Menus** and select the **Menu Video** tab.
2. Tap **Upload New Video** and choose an MP4 video (up to 100 MB).
3. Once the upload finishes, you will see an instant preview.
4. Tap **Save Changes**. The old video will be automatically cleaned up and replaced with your new one.

---

## 6. Downloading & Printing Table QR Codes

You don't need to hire a designer to create QR codes for your tables:
1. Go to **QR Codes** in your dashboard.
2. You will see your active QR codes (Main Menu and Special Menu).
3. Next to any QR code, you can download:
   - **PNG Image**: High-resolution image to give to your local print shop.
   - **Print-Ready PDF**: A beautifully styled table-tent flyer that you can print directly on any standard printer and place on your dining tables!
4. **Temporary Pause**: If you ever need to pause your digital menu, simply toggle the switch to **Disabled**. Customers who scan will see a polite "Menu Temporarily Offline" page directing them to your staff.

## 7. Updating Website Photos, Contact Info & FSSAI Number

You can keep your public website updated without calling a web developer:

### Updating Photos in the Gallery
1. Go to **Website** > **Gallery**.
2. Tap **+ Add Photo** to upload high-quality pictures of your restaurant's ambience, food, or kitchen.
3. You can drag and drop or use the arrows to reorder photos.

### Updating Popular Dishes on the Homepage
1. Go to **Website** > **Popular Dishes**.
2. You can choose any dish from your menu to feature prominently on the front page of your website.

### Changing Phone Numbers, Hours & Address
1. Go to **Website** > **Contact & Social**.
2. Update your phone number, WhatsApp number, opening hours, or Google Maps link.
3. Tap **Save**. Your website footer and contact page update immediately.

### Updating Your FSSAI Licence Number
1. Go to **Website** > **Footer & FSSAI**.
2. Enter your current 14-digit FSSAI licence number.
3. Tap **Save**. It will display in the legal footer of your website.

## 8. Boosting Google Reviews with AI

Your website includes a smart review assistant that makes it effortless for satisfied guests to leave you glowing Google reviews.

### How Customers Use It:
1. The guest taps 5 stars on their phone.
2. They tap quick highlight chips (like *"Authentic Taste"*, *"Fast Service"*, *"Pure Veg Excellence"*).
3. The AI instantly drafts a warm, personalized review in seconds.
4. The guest taps **Submit**, which automatically copies the review text and opens your Google Reviews page so they can paste it with one click!

### Managing Review Options in Your Dashboard:
1. Go to **Reviews** > **Options**.
2. You can add new chips (e.g., *"Crispy Bhakri"*, *"Great Family Ambience"*).
3. **Google Review Link**: Under **Reviews** > **Google Link**, you can test and update the link where customers are sent. Tap **Test Link** to make sure it opens your exact Google Business listing.

## 9. Account Settings & Changing Passwords

### To Change Your Email or Password:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Account**.
2. Enter your current password for security verification.
3. Enter your new email or new password and tap **Save Account**.

### What to Do If You Forget Your Password:
If you ever lock yourself out or forget your password, contact your technical support or agency. They can securely reset your password in 30 seconds using the server CLI tool without touching your restaurant data.

---

## 10. Exporting Your Restaurant Data

You always own your data. To download records for your accountant or records:
1. Go to **Settings** > **Data Export**.
2. Choose what you want to download:
   - **Download All Content**: A complete backup of your entire menu, dishes, gallery, and settings.
   - **Export QR Scans**: A spreadsheet (CSV) showing customer scan dates, times, and device types.
   - **Export Review Stats**: A spreadsheet showing your review funnel numbers.
