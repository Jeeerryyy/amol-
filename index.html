Our Heritage

Pure Vegetarian Excellence, Time-Honored Tradition

Since opening our doors at Old Pune Naka in 1990, Amol Veg Restro has remained dedicated to serving the finest pure vegetarian delicacies in Solapur. We believe dining should be an experience of comfort, celebration, and uncompromising hygiene.

From freshly ground whole spices and slow-cooked tomato-cashew gravies to our crispy golden sandwiches and refreshing thick cold coffees, each dish is prepared with pride in a strictly vegetarian kitchen.

✓ 100% Pure Vegetarian Kitchen (No meat, eggs, or gelatin)
✓ Fresh dairy cottage cheese (Paneer) sourced daily
✓ Jain food preparations prepared separately upon request
